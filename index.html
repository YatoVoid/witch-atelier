Sigil

Nothing to pick here. Draw a stroke anywhere on the circle and its shape decides what it is: straight and outward is a push, straight and inward is a pull, a wide sweep spreads, a tight scribble crushes. A pause of over a second lets you add more strokes to the same sign before it locks in, since most of these are drawn from a few parts, not one line.

New here? You can teach the sign reader your handwriting before you start. Draw each named sign a couple of times (a few minutes) and it'll match your style, and which sign you mean within a family, from the start -- not just after correcting it a bunch of times.
